Privacy

When you share a dish, we keep your photo, first name, state or province, your one-line story and your email.

We show only your photo, first name, state or province and story, and only after you tick the box that allows it. Your email is never shown or sold. We use it to tell you when your photo is pinned and, if you asked for it, to send the Sunday Supper Letter. Every letter has an unsubscribe link.

To remove your photo or your email, write to minimalistbites@gmail.com.
