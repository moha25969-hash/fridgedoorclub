Depression-Era Bread Pudding

Nothing wasted, everything loved. Stale bread, a little milk and sugar, and the whole kitchen smells like Sunday.

Prep 15 minBake 40–45 minServes 6

What you'll need

  • 6 cups day-old bread, in cubes
  • 1/2 cup raisins
  • 2 cups warm milk
  • 3 eggs
  • 1/2 cup sugar
  • 1 tsp cinnamon
  • 1 tsp vanilla
  • Butter for an 8x8 dish

How to make it

  1. Heat the oven to 350°F and butter an 8x8 baking dish.
  2. Spread the bread cubes and raisins in the dish.
  3. Whisk the warm milk, eggs, sugar, cinnamon and vanilla.
  4. Pour it over the bread and let it soak for 10 minutes.
  5. Bake 40 to 45 minutes, until golden and set in the middle. Serve warm, with a little cream.
