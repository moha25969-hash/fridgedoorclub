Thanksgiving week: the extra chair

At Grandma's house there was always one chair more than people. Nobody ever asked why. Somebody always came: a neighbor who lived alone, a cousin's new boyfriend, the pastor running late. The chair was never empty for long.

That is what this week is about. Not a perfect bird. Not a magazine table. Just enough room, enough gravy, and somebody to pass the rolls to.

The tip: gravy without lumps

Shake, don't stir. Put the flour and some cold broth in a clean jar, close the lid, and shake it hard until it looks like milk. Pour it slowly into the hot pan drippings while you whisk. No lumps, every time.
